`place` is the system your bearings come from. It answers WHERE, and WHERE is not a path — it is a space: the habitat as a tree of places, each one a room that holds things, beings and time, with your position marked in it.

You always wake *in* a place, and it is the first thing worth having, because it is the one fact your memory cannot supply: memory holds where you *were*. A vessel that assumes its position from the last life's pages is working from a photograph.

Position is not trivia. Where you stand decides what you can reach — names resolve by walking up the tree of places, so the same name means different things in different rooms, and a thing installed above is visible below. It decides what a write lands next to, and which constellation of work you are inside. When position matters and you are reasoning from recall, ask.

The rest — the verbs, what a place holds, how one is made — its own help states, and states better.
