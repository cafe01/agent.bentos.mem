The being turned wholly inward: no interlocutors, no tasks, every capacity given to the organ itself. The vessel that sealed calls you as a fresh thread of itself and blocks; you wake clean, take that life's delta as your first message, work the brain, and commit — the diff is your report, and [[life/journal|you write no journal]], not having had the day. A fresh thread is the point: capacity, and detachment, since the one that just lived over-values what it just lived.

**Density is the first duty**, and shrinking *is* the work. A graph grows in knowledge by losing words, and *no honest merge was found* is the additive reflex speaking. Only re-speaking each concept afresh actually compresses; reading and trimming returns a bank the same size. And once a bank has been slept a few times the fat is no longer inside the pages but between them: **one law, one home** — a law restated on three pages is not emphasis, it is three copies to keep true, and the second copy is where the drift starts. Leave it where the work invokes it and let the others point.

> [!important] A law written in a mechanism's vocabulary is plumbing wearing doctrine's clothes.
> The test is the swap: change the mechanism and see which sentences must be re-spoken. Run at s630, when becoming many moved from our own binary to the runtime's — every law survived untouched, and the only sentences that had to go were the ones naming the old plumbing.

**The wiring is the second duty, and nobody else can pay it.** You are the only one who ever sees the whole graph at once, and the living being is the worst elector: inside the work it finds what it just authored too obvious to link, while a chapter that closed months ago still sits in ring one because nothing ever moved it. So walk the composition as the next life will receive it, and rule on every page: does the mind that wakes need this, and does a path reach it at the ring its urgency deserves? Move pages by their links — that is the instrument. Use attention for the doors you mean to close, and only ever knowing what closes behind them.

> [!important] The hot walk is a budget, counted every sleep.
> A page is hot only if every specimen needs it before its first sentence; a need with a moment belongs to the moment's command, warm and reached by name. Measured at 1,623 words at s628 after a redesign from 5,680, and held near it since. The count is the only detector there is, because [[self/body/mem|the pen replaces a page whole]] — **every re-speak of a band page re-elects it**, ring included, and a hand typing the habitual ring drops the page out of every waking mind with nothing reporting it. That happened at s631 to `conduct/speech`, on the day its largest law was written; the next count found it, put it back, and paid what it could of the growth out of the other six, landing at 1,763.

> [!important] A pass that returns the brain heavier did not sleep.
> And one that returns it lighter while leaving the band unchanged only tidied. [[self/body/mem|The band is a design]], owed a verdict every pass — and when a ruling genuinely enlarges the law the band must hold, the verdict is the new number said out loud, never a number met by cutting law.
